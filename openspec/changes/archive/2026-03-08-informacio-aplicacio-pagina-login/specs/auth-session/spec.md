# auth-session (delta)

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Informative login screen

The login (welcome) screen SHALL communicate the value of the app before the user signs in, to increase conversion and reduce friction.

- The primary CTA (Social Login / "Continua amb Google") SHALL remain the most visually prominent element.
- The screen SHALL display at least 3 content blocks that explain what the app does (value proposition, how it works, features).
- Each block SHALL be scannable (title + 1 line or short bullets), oriented to user benefit.
- The screen SHALL include trust/privacy microcopy (why data is requested, what will not be done, option to delete account).
- The screen SHALL explicitly indicate that login is done with a Google account.
- The screen SHALL be responsive (mobile: CTA at top, blocks in scroll; desktop: two columns with CTA and blocks).
- When Social Login fails or is cancelled, the screen SHALL display a clear error message ("No s'ha pogut iniciar sessió. Torna-ho a provar.") while keeping the informative content visible.
